# concepts

Competing creative concepts for the site, kept separate from confirmed
product truth (`PRODUCT.md`) and raw references (`references/`).

Every file here is a **hypothesis**, not a decision, and must carry a
`STATUS:` line at the top saying so until `art-director` and the client
have actually chosen a direction. Do not treat the earliest-written
concept as a default or as something later concepts merely refine —
each one competes on equal footing.

- `INITIAL-HYPOTHESIS.md` — "From our decks to your deck." Proposed
  during product definition, before any art direction review. Unapproved.
