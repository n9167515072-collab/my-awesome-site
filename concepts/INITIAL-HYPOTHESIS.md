# Concept — "From our decks to your deck"

Proposed during product definition, before any art direction work began.
This is one early hypothesis for the site's central creative idea, not a
decision. It has not been reviewed by `art-director`, has not competed
against alternative directions, and must not be treated as a starting
point future concepts merely refine.

## Central creative concept

The site's single physical hero object is one Tarot card, which carries
the entire narrative through a continuous scroll sequence:

1. **Scene 1** — A calm, dark/neutral space. One card floats, face down.
   It reacts subtly to mouse/device tilt; foil catches real-looking light.
   On scroll, it flips: "Мы создаём колоды." ("We make decks.")
2. **Scene 2** — Camera pulls back. Dozens more cards are revealed behind
   the first, fanning out across the screen — these are real LunaLum
   works.
3. **Scene 3** — A journey through the portfolio in a spatial, 3D-feeling
   arrangement: Алиса → Вихри Света → Русские сказки → other decks. Each
   selected card advances into its own full showcase.
4. **Scene 4** — The physical object payoff: artwork becomes a real
   printed card, a box appears, cards fold into it; foil, edge gilding,
   material/print detail are shown up close.
5. **Scene 5** — After the last showcase, every shown card returns to
   center, reassembles into one deck, turns — and turns entirely blank.
   Empty box, empty cards. Copy: «Мы сделали свои.» (pause) «Теперь сделаем
   твою.» → CTA: «ХОЧУ СВОЮ КОЛОДУ».

Stated intent: overwhelm the visitor with existing work first, then
literally take it back and leave them an empty deck — theirs to fill.

Client's own synthesis, specific to realizing this concept: **Caeli +

## Content sections as envisioned by this hypothesis

The underlying content requirements (a hero, a showcase of own decks, a
process explanation, capabilities, production proof, a short about
section, a final CTA) were part of the original product brief and will
likely persist regardless of which creative direction is chosen. The
specific execution below, however, is this hypothesis's proposal for
realizing them — not confirmed:

1. **Hero** — minimal text, one very strong physical object (a deck or
   single card) reacting to the cursor, foil catching light. Copy:
   «Создадим твою колоду.»
2. **Showcase of LunaLum's own decks** — not a standard grid; each deck as
   its own full-screen scene (box → opens → cards fan out → one card
   approaches → foil detail → real product photo). Each deck changes the
   page's whole visual world.
3. **From idea to physical object** — sketch → art → card → deck → box →
   production → finished product, shown as a visual transformation rather
   than a 6-icon infographic.
4. **Capabilities** — Tarot, Oracle, custom deck structures, illustration,
   guidebook, box, foil, gilded edge, unusual materials, inserts, QR/digital
   mechanics.
5. **Production proof** — real photos of cards in hand, boxes, printing,
   samples, production; explicitly show the digital-render → real-object
   transition.
6. **Short "About LunaLum"** — a few trust-building facts, explicitly not a
   multi-screen company history.
7. **Final CTA** — after the full portfolio: «Следующая может быть твоей.»
   → «Хочу свою колоду».
