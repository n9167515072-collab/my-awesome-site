# Creative direction brief — LunaLum Deck Studio

This captures the visual/narrative concept and references the client gave
during product definition. Unlike `PRODUCT.md`, none of this is durable
product truth — it's a proposed creative direction to pressure-test (e.g.
via the `art-director` subagent and the `frontend-design` skill) before
committing to it in `DESIGN.md`/`new-work`. Treat it as a strong starting
hypothesis, not a locked decision.

## Central creative concept: "From our decks to your deck"

The site's single physical hero object is one Tarot card, which carries
the entire narrative through a continuous scroll sequence:

1. **Scene 1** — A calm, dark/neutral space. One card floats, face down.
   It reacts subtly to mouse/device tilt; foil catches real-looking light.
   On scroll, it flips: "Мы создаём колоды." ("We make decks.")
2. **Scene 2** — Camera pulls back. Dozens more cards are revealed behind
   the first, fanning out across the screen — these are real LunaLum
   works.
3. **Scene 3** — A journey through the portfolio in a spatial, 3D-feeling
   arrangement: Алиса → Вихри Света → Русские сказки → other decks. Each
   selected card advances into its own full showcase.
4. **Scene 4** — The physical object payoff: artwork becomes a real
   printed card, a box appears, cards fold into it; foil, edge gilding,
   material/print detail are shown up close.
5. **Scene 5** — After the last showcase, every shown card returns to
   center, reassembles into one deck, turns — and turns entirely blank.
   Empty box, empty cards. Copy: «Мы сделали свои.» (pause) «Теперь сделаем
   твою.» → CTA: «ХОЧУ СВОЮ КОЛОДУ».

Stated intent: overwhelm the visitor with existing work first, then
literally take it back and leave them an empty deck — theirs to fill.

## Reference sites (visual/technical direction, not competitors)

Deliberately drawn from strong physical-product sites, not from other
Tarot brands:

- **Lusion** — https://lusion.co — main reference for a large 3D object as
  the page's central character: object reacts to cursor, camera moves,
  one product state continuously morphs into another.
- **Caeli Energie** — on-scroll product presentation — key structural
  reference: the product doesn't disappear after the hero, it travels with
  the visitor through the whole page, changing as they scroll.
- **0110 Studio** — 3D scroll animation — reference for bolder spatial
  scenes; cards existing in 3D space with the camera moving between them
  rather than sitting flat on a page.
- **Plus X Digital Showroom** — reference for a "digital exhibition" feel;
  each LunaLum deck could be its own small world with its own background,
  typography, and atmosphere.
- **Organimo** — product experience — surreal presentation of an ordinary
  physical object; suggests a card could become a door, a space, or a
  transition between sections.
- **Zero Tech by Lusion** — reference for the balance between showy
  realtime 3D and a functioning commercial site — explicit warning from
  the client: don't build a tech demo a tarot reader can't read as "make
  me a deck."
- **Apple (AirPods Pro page)** — reference for a more realistic, disciplined
  execution: large product, sticky scenes, scale/angle changes,
  progressive detail reveal — achievable with pre-rendered sequences
  rather than heavy realtime 3D.
- **Boba (product slider)** — reference for transitions between products:
  the box stays roughly centered while artwork, environment, color, and
  type morph around it as you move between decks (e.g. Алиса → Вихри
  Света → Русские сказки).

Client's own synthesis for execution priority: **Caeli + Lusion for
spatial/continuous scroll storytelling, Apple for discipline, product
clarity, and mobile adaptation.**

## Explicit anti-references (what to avoid)

- Printing-house sites in general.
- The standard layout: "Услуги / Преимущества / Этапы работы / Отзывы /
  Форма заявки" (services / benefits / process steps / testimonials /
  contact form) as a generic template.
- Marketplace-style product card grids.
- Typical/stereotypical esoteric design language.
- Overly complex 3D sites that turn a commercial landing page into a game
  and perform poorly on mobile.

## Required content sections (as briefed, not yet a page structure)

1. **Hero** — minimal text, one very strong physical object (a deck or
   single card) reacting to the cursor, foil catching light. Copy:
   «Создадим твою колоду.»
2. **Showcase of LunaLum's own decks** — not a standard grid; each deck as
   its own full-screen scene (box → opens → cards fan out → one card
   approaches → foil detail → real product photo). Each deck changes the
   page's whole visual world.
3. **From idea to physical object** — sketch → art → card → deck → box →
   production → finished product, shown as a visual transformation rather
   than a 6-icon infographic.
4. **Capabilities** — Tarot, Oracle, custom deck structures, illustration,
   guidebook, box, foil, gilded edge, unusual materials, inserts, QR/digital
   mechanics.
5. **Production proof** — real photos of cards in hand, boxes, printing,
   samples, production; explicitly show the digital-render → real-object
   transition.
6. **Short "About LunaLum"** — a few trust-building facts, explicitly not a
   multi-screen company history.
7. **Final CTA** — after the full portfolio: «Следующая может быть твоей.»
   → «Хочу свою колоду».
